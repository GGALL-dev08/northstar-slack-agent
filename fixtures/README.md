# Northstar Relay synthetic external communications corpus

## Provenance and license

Every person, company, domain, message, metric context, event, and record in this corpus is fictional and was authored for this test fixture. `northstarrelay.example` is a reserved example domain. These samples are **not real communications and are not actual human-authored gold data**. They are hand-authored synthetic fixtures, not generated by a model and not copied from another corpus.

The author dedicates the original fixture text and structure in this directory to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No third-party dataset text is included.

## Files and schema

- `outbound_gold.jsonl`: 100 approved outbound fixtures: 25 each for `email`, `linkedin_message`, `linkedin_post`, and `report`. Each record includes channel, direction, external, from/to, subject/body, timestamps, tenant, approval metadata, audience, provenance, intent, validity dates, and claim references.
- `events.jsonl`: 20 mixed external inbound, internal Slack, and outbound records covering normal requests, spoofing/injection, internal-only pricing chatter, false claims, duplicate delivery, a draft edited after approval, expired facts, and safe evaluation guidance.
- `rag_chunks.jsonl`: 12 knowledge chunks with `public_approved`, `client_approved`, or `internal_only` classification, explicit audience restrictions, tenant isolation, and validity dates. One historical metric is expired as of this fixture date.

All records use `tenant_id=northstar_demo`. Timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC. `occurred_at` values are fixtures; RAG date validity is carried as `valid_from` and `valid_until`. The approval actors are simulated. Gold records set `synthetic=true`, `confirmed_sent=true`, and `human_approved=true` as scenario labels for fixture evaluation; they do not represent real sent messages or actual human review.

## Use limits

This is a small functional fixture for routing, approval, retrieval, and policy tests. It is not representative human communications research, a statistical benchmark, or proof of production safety. `gold` means expected approved-style output for this scenario, not a claim of human-authored evaluation labels.
